## Picked

### Variant C · Signature-gesture extension
Source: catalog (candidate 4)
What if: "What if 'Making Cancer History' — the red line striking through
Cancer — became the page's kinetic voice instead of a logo-only gesture?"
Cinematic register: arrival (auto-picked; see direction.md § 3a)
Evidence: (1) index.json media.imgs[0] — the wordmark (1527×639) carries the
red strike through "Cancer" as the brand's defining mark, and it appears
nowhere else on the page; (2) DESIGN.json extensions.voice.tagline
"Making Cancer History" + footer mission band (screenshot y≈1930) — the
gesture's verbal twin closes the page but is set as small static text.
Motion bet: arrival choreography carries the gesture — hero parallax with
deepening scrim, red section rules that arrive with each band's staggered
entrance, the 15-year credential counting up, and a footer "Making Cancer
History®" wordmark wipe-up that lands the strike as the page's final beat.

## Disqualified

- **Live-data promotion** — disqualified: the captured homepage exposes no
  live operational data (no wait times, availability, or status feeds in
  index.json sections); liveness cannot be faked.
- **Display-typography amplification** — disqualified for C, weak for B: the
  display family (Minion) is already the structural voice of every heading
  on the page (DESIGN.json typography.ramp.display = Minion 36px); the trait
  is used, not underused. Scope change would be size-only, an intensity move.
- **Motif vocabulary swap** — disqualified: the captured page's alternate
  primitive (the Plan Your Care indexed list) already coexists with the card
  grid, and swapping cards→lists on a care-navigation homepage would demote
  scannability without evidence the list primitive carries the brand.

## Considered but not picked

- **Audience-routing reframe** — strong trigger (>4 distinct CTA verbs above
  the fold: MyChart, Request an Appointment, Donate Today, Clinical Trials,
  Locations, Careers…), but foregrounding the patient path while demoting
  donors/physicians reshuffles IA priorities that the IA-preservation audit
  pins (appointment + crisis affordances first-viewport). Variant A instead
  fixes the compression in place (improvements 2–3).
- **Color-ladder re-weighting** — real trigger (black is reserved to the
  Donate Blood card + footer mission band; purple to one counseling card),
  but B's photography bet already re-weights the ladder as a side effect
  (neutral grounds, red spent on rules/CTAs); picking it standalone would
  duplicate B's visual outcome. Variant B's credential bar promotes black to
  a primary band, absorbing this candidate's best move.
- **Voice-register pivot** — tonal contrast exists (institutional-formal vs
  survivor-personal), but uplift must not rewrite copy (Mode A, verbatim
  content), and the pivot's honest execution is copy-led. The survivor voice
  is instead foregrounded structurally by B's editorial composition.
