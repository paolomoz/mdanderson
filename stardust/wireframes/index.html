MD Anderson homepage — three photography-led directions

Low-fidelity wireframes. Same content and brand (red #da291c, Making Cancer History®), three different registers. Photos are current-site images shown desaturated as placeholders — final art direction comes in the prototype phase. Red annotation tags on each page explain the intent of every structural decision.

Direction A

The Front Page

Editorial newspaper register: serif display, ink rules, press-stamp badge. The homepage reads like the front page of a trusted publication — authority through journalism, humanity through documentary photography.

  • Hero = one dominant story photo + banner headline
  • Highlights become a real front-page story grid (no carousel)
  • Red used as “ink”: one full red band (#EndCancer), rules and kickers elsewhere
Refero refs: Luffu (serif warmth), Christopher Ireland / Cori Corinne (editorial structure)

Direction B

Horizon

Cinematic and immersive: a 100vh photographic hero, floating glass nav, and a chaptered scroll (Your care → Why → Stories → Take action) with a sticky index rail. Boldest departure from the current site.

  • Care-finder search panel breaks the hero edge — single task entry point
  • Stories as a draggable full-height filmstrip
  • Dark stat interlude gives the #1 / 15-years numbers real weight
Refero refs: Superpower (full-bleed portrait + red CTA), until (glass nav, scroll cue), Inversa (anchored type)

Direction C

Care Canvas

Warm, humanist, task-first — the most Lilly-adjacent. Ivory canvas, rounded shapes, arch photo crops, sunlit imagery. Feels like a guide that takes your hand, not an institution.

  • “How can we help you today?” action bar overlaps the hero
  • Arch/circle photo crops as a signature brand shape
  • Soft red-tinted callouts keep urgency kind (blood drive)
Refero refs: August Health (people-centered crops), Function Health (warm canvas), Lilly.com (task-first humanism)
