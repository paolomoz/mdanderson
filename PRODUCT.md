# PRODUCT — mdanderson.org (descriptive, preserve-mode target)

> Replica target spec. Descriptive of the current live site; not a creative
> brief. Every statement traces to captured evidence.

## Register

`brand` — a public-facing institutional marketing/care-navigation site
(hero claims, campaign CTAs, donation funnels), with deep informational
content underneath.

## Product Purpose

UT MD Anderson Cancer Center's primary web property: route patients and
families to care (appointments, cancer types, clinical trials, locations),
carry the institution's research/education story, and drive philanthropy
(Give Now, Donate Blood, volunteer). Mission line (captured, footer): "to
eliminate cancer in Texas, the nation, and the world."

## Users

Patients and family members (dominant nav position), referring physicians,
researchers/trainees, donors and volunteers.

## Brand Personality (captured surface)

Authoritative and credentialed (US News "#1 in the nation for cancer care"
hero, NCI/NCCN badges), humane and survivor-voiced (Cancerwise stories,
patient quotes), urgent-but-hopeful campaign voice ("Help #EndCancer",
"Making Cancer History®").

## Design Principles (as observed)

- MD Anderson red (#DA291C) as the single dominant brand accent on
  black/white/gray neutrals; purple/blue/green as section accent chips.
- Serif editorial voice (Minion) for display and body; Univers sans for
  wayfinding (nav, labels, buttons).
- Square corners, flat surfaces, full-bleed banded layout on a 1440px
  container.
- Every page ends in the same conversion stack: Help #EndCancer trio →
  newsletter bar → mega footer.
