UT MD Anderson's Nellie B. Connally Breast Center is named for a fierce breast cancer advocate and former First Lady of Texas. With Nellie in mind, we strive to treat each breast cancer patient with personalized attention, the best diagnosis and treatment options, and follow-up care to maximize your life after cancer.

With locations across the Houston area, including the Texas Medical Center, League City, Sugar Land, West Houston and The Woodlands, UT MD Anderson has one of the largest breast cancer treatment programs in the world. This gives us a level of experience that is second to none. Our team of experts uses this knowledge to create highly specialized treatment plans for all types and stages of breast cancer.

UT MD Anderson's approach to breast cancer is more than just treatment. The Breast Center offers genetic testing and counseling for women with a family history of breast cancer. Your care team also includes skilled plastic surgeons that can make you look and feel normal again with the latest breast reconstruction procedures.

Breast cancer surgery: 4 things to know

We treat all types of breast cancer, including:

  • Ductal carcinoma in situ (DCIS)
  • HER2-positive breast cancer
  • Hormone receptor-positive/ER-positive breast cancer
  • Inflammatory breast cancer
  • Invasive lobular carcinoma
  • Male breast cancer
  • Metaplastic breast cancer
  • Paget disease of the breast
  • Triple-negative breast cancer
  • Breast cancers caused by BRCA genetic mutations
  • Breast cancer during pregnancy
